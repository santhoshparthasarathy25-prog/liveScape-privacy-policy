LiveScape — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

LiveScape (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use LiveScape mobile applications and related services (“Apps”).

Important: This policy is intended to describe the LiveScape Apps accurately. App-specific disclosures shown in Google Play and within an App take precedence where they provide more detail.

1. Information we collect

LiveScape does not ask you to create an account to use its wallpaper features. The Apps are designed to work primarily on your device. Information you choose to provide to us when contacting support (such as your email address and message) is used only to respond to your request.

Some Apps may need access to device features or content you choose, such as photos or media, so that you can select or display your own wallpaper. Such content is used for the feature you request. Where supported, it is kept on your device and can be removed by deleting it within the App or uninstalling the App.

2. How information is used

Information you send to support is used to answer your enquiry, troubleshoot issues, and maintain the service. Device permissions are used only to provide the feature for which you grant permission.

3. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own advertising or marketing. If an App version integrates a third-party service, its operation and data handling will be described in the relevant App-specific disclosure and the provider’s privacy policy.

4. Data storage and security

Wallpaper settings and media selected for wallpaper features may be stored locally on your device. We use reasonable measures for information received through support, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Children's privacy

LiveScape Apps are not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

6. Your choices and permissions

You can manage app permissions through your Android device settings. You can stop using an App and uninstall it at any time. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of information you sent directly to us, subject to applicable law.

7. Third-party services and links

An App may rely on Android, Google Play, or other services provided by third parties. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review their policies for details.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our Apps or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with the effective date updated above.
